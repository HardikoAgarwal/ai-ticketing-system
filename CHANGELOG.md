# Changelog

## Unreleased: Helpdesk AI rewrite (from the BookLeaf author portal)
### Changed
- Article editor, round six: no card header. Above the card, two neutral tabs: **How it works** (what the AI reads) and **Writing tips**. Inside the card, top to bottom: a large **Select category for article** select, **Who can use it** as three big cards (the earlier style is back), then the title and writing area. **Test AI search** and **Delete article** moved to a footer at the bottom of the card, with the word count and AI-search status on its right.
- Article editor, round five: the card now has two parts. The **header** holds everything you can do or set: AI preview and Writing tips (outlined buttons) on the left, Test AI search (the shared AI-bordered button) and a red outlined Delete on the right, then a row with a compact three-way **Who can use it** switch (Public, Staff only, Draft; the chosen one lifts onto a white pill with its colour, with a one-line explanation beside it) and a **Category** select with a tag icon. The **body** is just the title and the writing area. The "..." menu and the tab chips are gone; the visibility cards were replaced by the switch.
- Article editor, round four: the back button became a breadcrumb under the header (Knowledge base / article title). AI preview and Writing tips are tab-shaped chips on the card's top edge (blue and amber), with Test AI search (AI style) and the "..." menu on the right. Inside the card, Category is a labelled select with a hint and visibility is three equal cards (Public, Staff only, Draft) each with a coloured icon and a one-line explanation. The Writing tips and AI preview modals lost their header icons; AI preview now shows passage, word and longest-passage counts, a colour-coded bar showing how the article is split, and numbered passage cards.
- Tables: alternate rows are plain neutral shades, no colour: white and a near-white in light mode, two blacks in dark mode (`--row-alt`).
- Article editor layout: a soft pill "Knowledge base" back button at the top; AI preview and Writing tips as chips on the left and Test AI search (plus the "..." menu) on the right, above the card; inside the card, labelled Category and Visibility fields (with their hints) sit above a separator, then the title and writing area.
- Page headers no longer show the workspace name above the title. Article editor: the header is just the title and description; category, visibility (with its one-line explanation) and the AI preview / Writing tips / Test AI search buttons sit in a row below it, outside the card, and the article card with the title and writing area comes after.
- Knowledge base, round two. The Knowledge page has no tabs now: search, category filter, How it works, Test AI search, Categories and New article are all in the header (it puts them on their own row under the title until the screen is very wide), then the visibility tiles, then the table. **How it works** opens a modal that walks through the five steps and what Public, Staff only and Draft mean. **Test AI search** opens an AI-styled modal (turning aurora border and glow). The article editor lost its side card and the line under the title: category and visibility are a slim bar above the title (visibility is a three-way selector with a one-line explanation), and **AI preview**, **Writing tips** and **Test AI search** are header buttons that open modals.
- Knowledge base redesigned. Two tabs under one header: **Articles** (three visibility tiles that filter, search, a category filter, and the article table with title and excerpt, category, visibility, AI-search status and last updated; a collapsed "How the knowledge base powers AI drafts" note; a designed empty state) and **Test AI search**. Writing and editing are now one **full-page editor** (`/agent/knowledge/new`, `/agent/knowledge/:id`): a large title and writing area on the left; category, visibility, "How the AI will read this" (the passages) and writing tips in a side column; a save bar that appears when something changed; delete from a "..." menu with confirmation; discard confirmation when leaving with edits; read-only for agents. The two switches (Published, Internal) became one **Visibility** choice: Public, Staff only, Draft (same stored flags, no backend change). The old split-pane editor, list, stat tiles and overview were removed.
- Tables: the sticky header now stops a little below the top edge (a page-coloured band above it hides the rows passing underneath), and the alternate-row shading uses a theme-aware tint of the accent colour (`--row-alt`, defined for light and dark) instead of a grey, so it reads cleanly in both themes.
- Tables: alternate rows are faintly shaded so a long row is easy to follow (hover and selected tints still win); the last column (Updated / Rating) has space on its left as well as its right, in the header too, and its column is wider to fit.
- Select buttons: in tables the select control is a smaller rounded square placed further left; on cards the round select button on the left edge is now filled with the page colour (not the card colour) when empty.
- Tables: the coloured priority and category badges are back (status stays the quiet outlined label unless a ticket is overdue, at risk or has a customer waiting).
- Tables, round three (quieter): the badge now follows the ticket title; only things that need a person (overdue, at risk, a customer waiting) get a coloured badge, everything else is a neutral status label with a small coloured dot (no "Draft ready"); priority is a dot and a word; category is plain text; the unread dot is gone from the table; the first and last columns have more side padding.
- Tables, round two: the status (or attention) badge now sits beside the ticket title and the Status column is gone; the AI summary line is removed (reference only underneath); the header row is darker with stronger labels; rows hover and select with an accent tint that is clearly different from the page colour (the old hover matched the background in light mode); category shows from xl.
- Table view rebuilt as a real table on the Active and History pages: one white panel with a tinted header row that sticks while you scroll, hairline row dividers, a soft hover, and fixed column widths so the ticket column gets the room (category appears from 2xl, owner and time-to-resolve from xl). Rows are clickable and the subject is a real link; select is the round button in the first column. A matching table-shaped skeleton shows while loading. Below the large breakpoint, table view falls back to compact card rows. Shared `DataTable`, `Th`, `Td` in `components/ds`.
- Ticket history and auto-close. The dock now has a "Tickets" group with **Active** (open and in-progress work) and **History** (Resolved and Closed, `/agent/tickets/history`; phones get a History tab). History is a table with search, a date range, status tiles (All finished / Resolved / Closed) that filter it, time to resolve and rating, and it loads in pages; the ticket page's back link returns to History when you came from there. The Active list no longer offers Resolved or Closed in its Status filter. Backend: new workspace setting `auto_close_days` (default 7, 0 = off; Settings, Automation) that closes Resolved tickets with no customer reply, logged as "Closed automatically"; runs right after the setting is saved, about hourly while staff load the queue (durable job), once on every start (`manage.py auto_close_tickets`, also usable from a scheduler). New ticket list filters `resolved_after` / `resolved_before` and ordering by `resolved_at`. Migrations: `workspaces.0002`, `tickets.0002`.
- Tickets page v16: a ticket that arrives while you are looking at the list is marked with an animated "New" chip (pops in, pinging ring, breathing sparkle) on the top-right corner of its card or row, for 45 seconds; the purple card border and pulse are gone. Only tickets created in the last 10 minutes can be marked, so changing a filter, sort or view can never mark anything.
- Tickets page v15: removed the Critical stat tile (four tiles: open, overdue, unassigned, assigned to me; Critical is still a filter). Fixed the purple border and pulsing flash after changing a filter: the list wrongly treated the tickets that appeared in the new result as "just arrived" and highlighted them for 7 seconds; it now only highlights a ticket that really arrives while you are looking at the same list.

- Tickets page v13: more space between the page header, the stat tiles and the list; applied filters are plain chips with a Clear all button (no card, no ticket counts; the popup button now says Done); designed empty states for "no tickets match these filters" (with a Clear all filters button) and for an empty view ("Inbox zero", nothing assigned to you, every ticket has an owner); an end-of-list marker under the last ticket.
- Tickets page v12: the select-all box and "N tickets" row are gone; "Select all N" lives in the bulk dock that appears when you select a ticket. Filters popup rebuilt (sectioned cards with coloured options, count badges, Clear all and a "Show N tickets" button); the Filters button gets a count dot when anything is applied. Applied filters show as coloured chips with remove buttons, the match count and a Clear all button in a bar under the stat tiles. Coloured, shimmering skeletons for the stat tiles on load and for the cards / table rows whenever a tile, filter or sort changes the list; the list eases in when it arrives.
- Tickets page v11: a selected card (or table row) no longer gets a green border; it shows the filled check and a light green wash only.
- Tickets page v10: search (smaller), Filters, Sort and the Cards / Table switch now live in the page header (the "N tickets · N overdue" line is gone), followed by the stat tiles and then the tickets; applied filters show under the tiles. The ticket stub is wider and shows customer and owner names larger. A selected card shows a green border.
- Tickets page v9: "New message" (green) and "Overdue" (red) are tabs sitting on the top-left edge of a card, side by side, like the Back button tab; the unread dot and the amber badge are gone; cards have no border at all (the white frame around the stub is gone) and a selected card gets a green outline.

- Domain generalised from a publishing-specific author portal to a multi-workspace helpdesk: authors, books and royalties became requesters, generic records and configurable categories.

- AI pipeline now triages (category, priority, sentiment, summary), embeds tickets, and drafts from retrieved knowledge-base chunks with citations instead of a fixed YAML knowledge base.

### Added
- Workspaces with owner/admin/agent/requester roles, signup, invites, per-workspace categories, SLA hours, tone and AI toggle; non-members get 404.
- Knowledge-base articles with chunking, embeddings (keyword fallback), staff-only articles, a search endpoint, and requester self-service suggestions.
- Duplicate detection, sentiment, summaries, CSAT, macros, per-workspace analytics including AI override rate, cost and draft accept/edit rate.
- Free-tier AI budget caps (global and per workspace) and a live usage endpoint.
- Demo sandbox: one click creates an isolated, pre-filled workspace (SaaS, IT helpdesk, e-commerce templates) that expires after 24 hours; `build_demo_embeddings` and `purge_demos` commands.
- Inbound email webhook (`POST /api/v1/inbound/email/`, shared secret).
- Dockerfile, docker-compose, GitHub Actions CI and keep-alive workflow, regenerated OpenAPI schema, new README and architecture write-up.

### Removed
- Celery, Redis, channels-redis, the BookLeaf catalog app, royalty logic and sample data, `kb.yaml`.
