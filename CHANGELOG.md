# Changelog

## Unreleased: Helpdesk AI rewrite (from the BookLeaf author portal)
### Changed
- Tickets page v16: a ticket that arrives while you are looking at the list is marked with an animated "New" chip (pops in, pinging ring, breathing sparkle) on the top-right corner of its card or row, for 45 seconds; the purple card border and pulse are gone. Only tickets created in the last 10 minutes can be marked, so changing a filter, sort or view can never mark anything.
- Tickets page v15: removed the Critical stat tile (four tiles: open, overdue, unassigned, assigned to me; Critical is still a filter). Fixed the purple border and pulsing flash after changing a filter: the list wrongly treated the tickets that appeared in the new result as "just arrived" and highlighted them for 7 seconds; it now only highlights a ticket that really arrives while you are looking at the same list.
- Tickets page v14: stat tile colours now come from the same maps as the badges (the Critical tile is the Critical priority colour, Overdue matches the overdue tab, Open matches the Open status); the fade-in on the list, applied chips and empty states is gone (it made freshly loaded results look faded for a moment); when a filter, tile or sort changes the list, the old list stays until the new one arrives and the placeholder cards only appear if that takes more than about a third of a second.
- Tickets page v13: more space between the page header, the stat tiles and the list; applied filters are plain chips with a Clear all button (no card, no ticket counts; the popup button now says Done); designed empty states for "no tickets match these filters" (with a Clear all filters button) and for an empty view ("Inbox zero", nothing assigned to you, every ticket has an owner); an end-of-list marker under the last ticket.
- Tickets page v12: the select-all box and "N tickets" row are gone; "Select all N" lives in the bulk dock that appears when you select a ticket. Filters popup rebuilt (sectioned cards with coloured options, count badges, Clear all and a "Show N tickets" button); the Filters button gets a count dot when anything is applied. Applied filters show as coloured chips with remove buttons, the match count and a Clear all button in a bar under the stat tiles. Coloured, shimmering skeletons for the stat tiles on load and for the cards / table rows whenever a tile, filter or sort changes the list; the list eases in when it arrives.
- Tickets page v11: a selected card (or table row) no longer gets a green border; it shows the filled check and a light green wash only.
- Tickets page v10: search (smaller), Filters, Sort and the Cards / Table switch now live in the page header (the "N tickets · N overdue" line is gone), followed by the stat tiles and then the tickets; applied filters show under the tiles. The ticket stub is wider and shows customer and owner names larger. A selected card shows a green border.
- Tickets page v9: "New message" (green) and "Overdue" (red) are tabs sitting on the top-left edge of a card, side by side, like the Back button tab; the unread dot and the amber badge are gone; cards have no border at all (the white frame around the stub is gone) and a selected card gets a green outline.
- Tickets page v8: ticket cards have rounded corners again, no visible border (a soft two-layer shadow instead; a selected card gets a green border), and status, category, opened and updated are back as a structured, labelled row at the bottom of the card with the status as a badge.
- Tickets page v7: priority (High / Critical) moved into the ticket stub next to the reference; ticket cards have square corners. Attention badges (overdue, draft ready) and "New message" still hang off the top edge.
- Tickets page v6: much shorter ticket cards (wider stub with reference, barcode, customer and owner side by side; status and category tags back at the bottom of the card with the times). Badges take no room: "New message" (now a bright amber highlight, not dark) hangs off the top-left edge, priority and attention badges off the top-right edge. Selection is a round button on the left edge that fills green with a check; a selected card gets a green outline and a light green wash.
- Tickets page v5: AI triage removed from the list cards (it belongs in the ticket details); the stub is a stronger tint and carries the status badge; "New message" is a distinct dark pill with an envelope and a pulsing green dot (cards and table); a clean select box (empty rounded square, solid ink with a check when chosen, dash for select-all) sits in the stub corner, and a selected card gets a clean ink outline. `ai-halo` and `AiSurface animated` are kept for the ticket page.
- Tickets page v4: the list now scrolls with the page (one scrollbar for the whole page, no clipped shadows or inner scroll box; bulk bar is sticky). Ticket cards: light-tinted stub instead of a solid one, two big badges (attention + priority) top right, status/category/opened/updated as plain labelled facts, and the AI triage on a soft violet background with category, priority, sentiment and confidence as plain facts inside the glowing frame.
- Tickets page v3: bigger stat tiles (all white text; Unassigned is now violet), larger search, a tidier filter popup (status, priority, category; overdue and owner are the tiles) with solid colour options, Sort popup; "group by urgency" and row density removed. Ticket cards are drawn as tickets: main part with chips top right, a priority-coloured stub with reference, barcode, customer, owner and times behind a dashed perforation with notches, and the AI triage in a glowing, slowly turning aurora frame with a confidence meter and solid tags.
- Overview: "How long tickets have waited" is a bar chart again (one column per day waiting, green to red, median wait and oldest ticket as headlines), kept full width.
- Tickets page v2: five solid colour stat tiles that double as the list filters (the Attention / Mine / Unassigned / All switcher is gone; resolved tickets via Filters > Status), full-width ticket cards with a priority-coloured band, solid status/attention/category badges, a details panel, and a solid violet AI triage strip tucked underneath; table rows are spacious cards with solid badges. Overview: ticket age is now four plain solid tiles (count, meaning, share of queue). Shared `SolidChip` and `fill`/`on` helpers in `components/ds`.
- Tickets page: queue stats strip (open, overdue, unassigned, critical, mine; each filters the list), ticket cards (header with reference, status, attention, priority, category, subject, customer message, requester and owner) with the AI triage tucked underneath as a strip (summary, AI category/priority/sentiment, confidence, draft ready), and a Cards / Table switch (`?layout=table`). Header, stats and filters now scroll with the list. Overview: ticket age is now a dot timeline (one dot per open ticket, four zones from fresh to stale) and sits full width above the response-target gauge and workload tiles.
- Light theme background is now a warm white (`#f8f6f1` page, white panels) instead of cream; borders and inset tones retuned to match. Chart and KPI header icons use the emerald accent tint.
- Overview: KPI comparison fits on one line ("↗ 12% vs 31 last period"); priority ring is larger; category tiles have their colours back; ticket age is now a day-by-day histogram (green to red) with median wait and oldest ticket as headlines; workload is a grid of owner tiles with one dot per open ticket (red = overdue).
- Overview colours now carry meaning only: green/red for better/worse (deltas, backlog change, response targets, overdue), a calm-to-urgent scale for priority and ticket age, neutral tiles for categories and icons. Chart cards have a header band (icon, title, one-line description, headline figure). Ticket age is now columns with hints and an "oldest ticket" headline; workload is a full-width ranked list with overdue shown as a red tail. The AI assistance card was taken off Overview (component kept in `features/home/AiInsights.tsx` to be placed elsewhere).
- Shared page header (icon tile, workspace label, title, description, actions) on Overview, Tickets, Knowledge and Settings. Overview reworked: comparison line with the earlier figure on every KPI card, average times as "1d 2h", customer sentiment moved to the top, queue cards removed, full-width volume chart, a different chart type per stat (area, bar, ring, treemap, gauge, columns, ranked list) with labels inside the graphics, solid chart colours (`--viz-*`), table toggles removed, and a simpler AI card.
- Home is now an analytics-only Overview: big KPI cards with trend lines, volume chart, response-target and priority donuts, category/sentiment/ticket-age/workload bars. Chart labels are drawn on the charts themselves (no separate legends). Removed the desktop top bar; search, live status and the account menu live in the dock (phones keep a slim top bar). Ticket lists and activity feed removed from Home.
- Dashboard rebuilt as "Paper & Aurora" (see `docs/DESIGN.md`): warm paper theme with a designed dark mode, icon dock and Cmd-K command palette, split-view inbox with keyboard shortcuts, tabbed ticket workspace with full state handling, and a shared aurora glow reserved for AI features. Login, signup, invite, sandbox setup, knowledge base, settings, analytics and the customer portal were redesigned on the same design system (`components/ds`).
- Domain generalised from a publishing-specific author portal to a multi-workspace helpdesk: authors, books and royalties became requesters, generic records and configurable categories.
- Infrastructure simplified for permanent free hosting: Celery and Redis replaced by a Postgres-backed job table with an in-process pool; Channels uses the in-memory layer; cache is local memory. Render's Postgres/Redis blocks removed (`render.yaml` expects a Neon `DATABASE_URL`).
- AI pipeline now triages (category, priority, sentiment, summary), embeds tickets, and drafts from retrieved knowledge-base chunks with citations instead of a fixed YAML knowledge base.

### Added
- Workspaces with owner/admin/agent/requester roles, signup, invites, per-workspace categories, SLA hours, tone and AI toggle; non-members get 404.
- Knowledge-base articles with chunking, embeddings (keyword fallback), staff-only articles, a search endpoint, and requester self-service suggestions.
- Duplicate detection, sentiment, summaries, CSAT, macros, per-workspace analytics including AI override rate, cost and draft accept/edit rate.
- Free-tier AI budget caps (global and per workspace) and a live usage endpoint.
- Demo sandbox: one click creates an isolated, pre-filled workspace (SaaS, IT helpdesk, e-commerce templates) that expires after 24 hours; `build_demo_embeddings` and `purge_demos` commands.
- Inbound email webhook (`POST /api/v1/inbound/email/`, shared secret).
- Dockerfile, docker-compose, GitHub Actions CI and keep-alive workflow, regenerated OpenAPI schema, new README and architecture write-up.

### Removed
- Celery, Redis, channels-redis, the BookLeaf catalog app, royalty logic and sample data, `kb.yaml`.
